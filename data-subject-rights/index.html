What data we collect

  • Voice recordings — audio files created by the user, stored locally on the device only.
  • Usage analytics — anonymised behavioural events (session start/end, feature usage) sent to Firebase Analytics.
  • Crash reports — anonymised crash traces sent to Firebase Crashlytics.
  • AI conversation history — text sent to OpenAI API to generate responses; not stored by us beyond the active session.

We do not sell, share, or transfer personal data to third parties for advertising purposes.

Your rights

  • Access — request a copy of personal data we hold about you.
  • Deletion — request erasure of your personal data.
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Export — request your data in a portable format.
  • Objection — object to processing of your data.

How to exercise your rights

Send an email to: bugtsa@gmail.com

Please include:

  • Subject line: Privacy Request — Emotion Sliv App
  • Request type: deletion / access / export / correction
  • Any identifiers that help locate your data (e.g. approximate date of first use)

Response time: 1 business day.

Local data deletion

Voice recordings are stored only on your device. You can delete them at any time from within the app, or by uninstalling the app.
